Positional versus dispositional returns

Universal quality improvements can reduce socioeconomic learning inequality even when both groups receive exactly the same improvement. Because low-SES children begin in lower-quality schools, diminishing marginal returns can give them a positional advantage. This positional advantage may be reinforced or offset by socioeconomic differences in conversion.
